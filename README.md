# Dynamic price table for private mobility energy component

## Possible applications: 
This is an useful reference for private mobility users to have up-to-date information on energy carrier prices and the average cost for a 100 km journey. Accessing to a specifically implemented ENEA web-app, the users will find the average unit prices for gasolin, diesel, LPG, methane, electricity and hydrogen. Moreover, by entering the average consumption of the car they are interested in, they will be able to obtain, in output, an economic comparison on the average costs for a 100 km journey, for each energy vectors chosen by the user.

## Description of the problem/need to be addressed:
At present, it is very difficult to find on a single website the average and up-to-date unit prices of all energy carriers for private mobility.

## Services provided description:
Users of private mobility will have an easy and immediate access to comprehensive up-to-date information on unit prices for all energy carriers. They will also be able to compare, in real time, the expenditure to cover 100 km, given the consumption, for each energy carrier, entered by the user himself.

## Service distinguishing features compared to existing solutions: 
It is the first web app that simultaneously compares three types of energy carriers, allowing to choose between various alternatives: traditional fuels, electric and electric/hydrogen. The frequency of updating the energy cost is daily for traditional fuels and quarterly for electric recharges. Also, for electric recharges, it distinguishes by power and charging location, offering a cost estimate closer to the user's needs.
